About Me

Hi, I'm Mugil.

Wildlife Photographer • Explorer • Experience Curator

Mugil KMV is a wildlife photographer and explorer with over 13 years of experience behind the camera. What began as a passion for wildlife photography gradually evolved into a deeper pursuit — exploring wild spaces, observing animals in their natural habitats, and sharing those experiences with like-minded people.

Why Wild Pixels

Photography built on patience, not luck.

Every frame here is earned in the field — no baiting, no staged shots, no shortcuts. Just time spent watching and waiting.

01

Patience Over Perfection

Hours in a hide or a jeep for a few honest seconds of behaviour — that's the trade this work is built on.

02

Ethical & Respectful

No disturbing wildlife for a frame. Distance, silence and the animal's comfort always come first.

03

Tours, Not Just Trips

Small groups, real field craft, and guidance on getting the shot — not just a seat in a safari jeep.
